Codeflaws supplies the original programs and tests used in the complete-source
study. The study screens the complete retained index and executes the admitted
programs with independent compilers. The archive, original translation units
and original test-file payloads are obtained separately for reproduction.
The delivered records retain their member identities, normalized scalar inputs,
observed results and experiment protocols. The 300-row metadata extract in
public-data/codeflaws-first-300.tsv belongs to a separate metadata control;
it is not the population of the complete-source study.

The normalized metadata extract comes from the Codeflaws repository's defect
index. Its row for 604-A-bug-14841012-14841153 appends an empty tag field to the
upstream three-field record. The exact repository MIT License, Copyright
(c) 2017 Shin Hwei Tan, is preserved in Codeflaws-MIT.txt. This notice accompanies
the bundled metadata. The source archive remains an external input; this
project does not make an archive-wide licensing determination.
